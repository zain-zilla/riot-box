GESTURE SYNTH HANDS

Play a synth with your bare hands in Meta Quest. Put the controllers down, then press Enter VR.

  • Right hand: pinch to play. Raise or lower your hand to change the note.
  • Left hand: height opens the filter. Pinch for a bass drone.
  • Hands apart: more reverb.
  • Make a fist (either hand) to start or stop the beat.

No headset? Hold the mouse button and move up/down to play, left/right for the filter. B = beat, mouse wheel = reverb.
