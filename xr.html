Play the synth in VR. Open this page in the Meta Quest browser and press Enter VR. Tap the glowing pads with your fingers (hand tracking), or point and pull the trigger. Slide the mixer faders above the pads (filter, resonance, drive, delay, reverb, octave, BPM, volume) with a fingertip, or point, hold the trigger and drag.

No headset? Click the pads with your mouse, or use keys A S D F G H J K (bottom row) and Q W E R T Y U I (top row).
